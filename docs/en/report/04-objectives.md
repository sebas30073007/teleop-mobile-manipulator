# Objectives

## General objective

Develop a remote-operation robotic system oriented toward last-mile logistics tasks in local distribution centers, enabling telework for people with motor disabilities.

## Specific objectives

**SO1 — Task analysis:** Identify and classify logistics tasks that can be executed remotely, considering their operational complexity, cognitive requirements, and physical constraints.

**SO2 — System requirements:** Establish the functional, operational, and accessibility requirements that a remote-operation system must meet to be usable by people with motor disabilities.

**SO3 — Technical integration:** Develop a teleoperated robotic system that integrates human-robot interaction, aligned with accessibility and logistics-task requirements.

**SO4 — Experimental validation:** Evaluate the designed system through usability metrics, comparing it against conventional logistics processes.
