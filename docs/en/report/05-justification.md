# Justification

The growing complexity of the logistics sector demands solutions that improve operational efficiency without increasing structural costs. At the same time, the low labor participation of people with disabilities responds, to a large extent, to physical and organizational barriers rather than to functional limitations.

Remote operation offers an opportunity to address both challenges, provided it is designed in a systematic and evaluable manner. This research is justified by the need to develop an accessible and viable remote-operation system that enables the integration of people with disabilities into logistics tasks.
