# Research Question

> **How can a remote-operation system be designed to allow people with motor disabilities to perform logistics tasks in local distribution nodes associated with the last mile?**

## Why this question is answerable through engineering

The question does not seek to establish a universal truth nor to compare the system against every possible alternative approach. Its scope is precise: to validate a specific solution under controlled conditions, with observable metrics and a reproducible evaluation protocol.

The methodological approach is experimental: a functional prototype is built, quantitative metrics are defined (time per task, error rate, usability score), comparative tests are run, and the results are analyzed against predefined operational hypotheses. This approach places the project within the *engineering design research* paradigm: artifact-oriented research, where knowledge is generated through the design, construction, and evaluation of real systems in controlled contexts. The question is not "does XR teleoperation work in general?" but "does *this* system, with *this* architecture, work for *these* tasks, with *these* users?"
