# Safety

## Risks to consider

- Electrical risks from the power supply (12–24 V DC) and power electronics
- Mechanical risks from movement of the mobile base and manipulator
- Risks from remote operation without physical confirmation of the environment
- Limited field of view while using the Meta Quest headset

## Current minimum recommendations

- Operate in a clear, delimited area
- Maintain in-person supervision during all tests
- Verify the immediate stop method before each session
- Test movements at low speed before full integration tests

> **Pending:** Formalize the safety protocol with the team: safe-stop procedure, pre-test checklist, operation zones, and minimum supervision distances.
